Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026 | NDPR & Data Protection Compliant

1. Data Transparency & Overview

At Ankabit Farm, protecting the privacy and personal data of our wholesale egg buyers in Nigeria is a top priority. This policy outlines how we handle and protect data submitted through our B2B trade application portal.

2. 2-Step Progressive Lead Capture Retention

To ensure zero lead drop-off and efficient wholesale support, our system automatically retains your Step 1 contact details (Full Name, Email, Phone Number) immediately upon submission:

  • Step 1 Contact Capture: Recorded instantly so our trade desk can follow up if your connection drops.
  • Step 2 Order Specs: Stores your requested crate quantity (minimum 100 crates), delivery state, and custom logistics notes.

3. How Your Data Is Used

Data is used exclusively to contact you regarding wholesale egg price quotes, dispatch truck scheduling across Nigerian states, and preventing automated bot spam. We do NOT sell or share your contact details.

4. Your Rights under NDPR

Under the Nigerian Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), you have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your saved contact records by emailing privacy@ankabitfarm.com.ng.
