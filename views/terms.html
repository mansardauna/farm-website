Ankabit Farm B2B Wholesale Agreement

1. Minimum Wholesale Order Quantity (100 Crates)

Ankabit Farm operates strictly as a commercial B2B supplier. The minimum allowable order size per transaction is 100 crates (equivalent to 3,000 fresh eggs). Orders below 100 crates will not be processed under wholesale pricing.

2. Pricing & Invoicing

Prices are quoted in Nigerian Naira (NGN) based on daily farm-gate rates and egg sizes (Medium, Large, Extra Large). Official waybills and invoices are issued upon dispatch.

3. Delivery & Transit Breakage Policy

We utilize shock-buffered crate packaging and padded delivery trucks. Buyers inspect crates at offloading alongside our transport driver. Verified transit breakage exceeding 1% will be credited or replaced in the subsequent dispatch.

4. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the commercial laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
