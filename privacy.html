Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026 | NDPR & Data Protection Compliant

1. Data Collection Notice

At Ankabit Farm, protecting your commercial trade data is paramount. When submitting wholesale egg supply inquiries, our system records your Step 1 contact information (Full Name, Business Email, WhatsApp Phone Number) immediately to ensure our sales desk can contact you even if your network connection drops.

2. How Data is Used

Your contact details are strictly used to coordinate farm-gate egg dispatch logistics, confirm minimum crate volume requirements (minimum 100 crates), and send confirmation receipts via email to olaomansur@gmail.com and your registered email address.

3. Contact

For data inquiries, contact our Privacy Desk at trade@ankabitfarm.com.ng or olaomansur@gmail.com.
