Commercial Wholesale Agreement | Ankabit Farm Nigeria

1. Wholesale Minimum Order Policy

Ankabit Farm operates strictly as a commercial B2B egg producer and distributor. The minimum allowable order size per transaction is 100 crates (3,000 fresh eggs, ~200 kg weight). Orders below 100 crates will not be processed for dispatch.

2. Logistics & Delivery Desk

All wholesale shipments are dispatched directly from automated layer packing facilities across Nigeria to destination markets in Lagos, Ogun, Oyo, Abuja, Enugu, Rivers, Kano, and nationwide.
